HOW IT WORKS:

1. AGGRESSION
   - Hold weapon + attack mob
   - Mob becomes hostile
   - Mob attacks you back
   - Trust drops

2. BUILDING LOYALTY
   - Feed with favorite food (+20-30 trust)
   - Use healing items (+25-100 trust)
   - Pet regularly (+3-5 trust per pet)
   - Talk positively
   - Never betray with weapons

3. COMBAT ALLIANCE
   - When trust > 80 AND mood is good
   - Mob will automatically defend you
   - Mob attacks your enemies
   - Mob fights alongside you

4. FORGIVENESS SYSTEM
   - If you betray a loyal mob
   - Use healing items to apologize
   - Feed them favorite food
   - Rebuild trust over time

TRUST RANGES:
-100 to 0:    HOSTILE/DISTRUSTFUL
0 to 50:      NEUTRAL
50 to 80:     FRIENDLY
80+ to 200:   LOYAL (WILL HELP!)

GAMEPLAY EXAMPLE:
1. /summon minecraft:cow → Bessie appears
2. /hold wheat
3. /mob feed → Bessie eats (trust: +20)
4. /mob pet → Bessie is petted (trust: +5)
5. /mob loyalty → Bessie: FRIENDLY
6. Keep feeding/petting...
7. /mob loyalty → Bessie: LOYAL - WILL DEFEND YOU!
8. Enemy approaches
9. Bessie attacks enemy automatically!
